## Design Context

### Users

Students and young professionals who want a warm, low-friction way to build recurring habits. They check the app in short sessions throughout the day and need progress, reminders, and history to remain useful without an account or network connection.

### Brand Personality

Gentle, focused, and playfully curious. The product celebrates effort without becoming noisy, childish, or competitive.

### Aesthetic Direction

"Check-in Little Planet" uses a deep-violet space identity, soft lavender surfaces, warm cream neutrals, and small yellow, mint, coral, and sky-blue status accents. A chibi astronaut and cat provide memorable moments while all controls, labels, charts, and progress states remain native SwiftUI. Light and dark appearances are first-class.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next check-in obvious and reachable in one tap.
2. Use illustration to reward or orient, never to cover data or controls.
3. Keep information architecture identical across iPhone and iPad; adapt density and navigation, not features.
4. Reserve saturated color and motion for meaningful progress, completion, and warnings.
5. Preserve Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, 44-point targets, and readable contrast throughout.
